description: "Add Velt from the Okta app catalog so your team signs in to the Velt Console with Okta, and let Okta add and remove people for you."
---

This guide sets up the **Velt** app from the Okta Integration Network (OIN). Your team then signs in to the Velt Console with Okta, and Okta can add and remove people in your Velt workspace.

It controls access to the Velt Console only, where you manage workspaces, API keys, webhooks and team members. It has no effect on the Velt SDK running in your product.

<Note>
Using another identity provider, or Velt isn't in your Okta catalog yet? Use the [manual SAML setup](/security/saml-sso) instead.
</Note>

## Supported features

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| IdP-initiated SSO | People click the Velt tile on their Okta dashboard and land in the Velt Console. |
| SP-initiated SSO | People start on Velt's sign-in page, enter their work email, and Velt sends them to Okta. |
| Create users | Assigning someone to the Velt app in Okta adds them to your Velt workspace as a Member. |
| Update user attributes | Okta sends profile changes and reactivations to Velt. |
| Deactivate users | Unassigning or deactivating someone in Okta removes them from your Velt workspace. |

Not supported:

- **Single Logout.** Signing out of Velt doesn't sign you out of Okta, and the reverse.
- **Just-in-time provisioning.** Signing in with Okta never adds anyone to a workspace. Each person needs an invite in Velt, or is added by user sync.
- **Group push and roles from Okta.** You can assign an Okta group to the app; Okta syncs its members one by one. Change roles in Velt under **Team → Users**.
- **Passwords.** The Velt Console doesn't use passwords, so there's nothing to sync.

## Prerequisites

- An Okta admin who can add apps (Super Administrator or Application Administrator).
- Owner or admin access to your Velt workspace. Only owners and admins see **Team → SAML**.
- Your company email domain, for example `acme.com`. Everyone who signs in with SSO needs an email on this domain. You can't use public email domains such as `gmail.com`.
- For user sync: Okta Lifecycle Management on your Okta plan. Without it, invite people in Velt under **Team → Users**.

## Set up sign-in

<Steps>
<Step title="Set up your workspace in Velt">

Sign in to the [Velt Console](https://console.velt.dev), open **Team → SAML**, and choose **App-based**. Under **1. Your workspace**, fill in:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Display Name | Your company name, for example `Acme`. People see it on your sign-in page. |
| Email Domain | Your company email domain without `@`, for example `acme.com`. A subdomain such as `mail.acme.com` counts as a different domain. |
| URL Slug | A short name for your workspace, for example `acme`. Use lowercase letters, numbers and dashes. You type it into Okta in the next step. |

</Step>
<Step title="Add Velt in Okta">

In the Okta Admin Console, go to **Applications → Applications → Browse App Catalog**. Search for **Velt**, open it, and click **Add Integration**.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Application label | A name your team will recognize, for example `Velt`. |
| Velt workspace URL slug | The URL Slug from step 1. Velt shows it with a copy button under **2. Add Velt in Okta**. |
| Self-hosted Velt API URL | Leave empty. It's only for self-hosted Velt deployments. |

Click **Done**. On the **Sign On** tab, set **Application username format** to **Email**, or keep **Okta username** if your Okta usernames are email addresses. Velt identifies people by their email.

</Step>
<Step title="Copy the metadata URL">

On the app's **Sign On** tab, copy the **Metadata URL**. It looks like `https://acme.okta.com/app/…/sso/saml/metadata`.

</Step>
<Step title="Connect Okta in Velt">

Back on **Team → SAML → App-based**, paste the link into **3. Connect Okta → Metadata URL from Okta** and click **Import**. Velt fills in Okta's Entity ID, SSO URL and signing certificate, and shows when the certificate expires.

Click **Save Configuration**. Velt shows **SAML configuration saved.**

</Step>
<Step title="Assign people in Okta">

On the app's **Assignments** tab, click **Assign → Assign to People** or **Assign to Groups**. Only people assigned to the app can sign in to Velt with Okta.

</Step>
<Step title="Give people access in Velt">

Okta decides who can sign in. Your Velt workspace decides who gets in: only its members can open it. Add people one of two ways:

- **Invite each person** in Velt under **Team → Users**, with the email they use in Okta. Works with any Okta plan.
- **Sync from Okta**, so Okta adds people when you assign them and removes them when you unassign them. See [Set up user sync](#set-up-user-sync).

</Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
To check it works, open a private browser window, sign in to Okta as someone you assigned and gave access, and click the Velt tile. You land in your workspace in the Velt Console.
</Tip>

### Sign in from Velt (SP-initiated SSO)

1. Go to [console.velt.dev/saml/login](https://console.velt.dev/saml/login), or your workspace's sign-in page, `https://console.velt.dev/saml/<slug>/login`.
2. Enter your work email and click **Continue with SSO**. If more than one workspace uses SSO for your email domain, choose yours from the list.
3. Okta signs you in and sends you back to the Velt Console.

The direct sign-in link, `https://console.velt.dev/saml/<slug>/sso`, skips the email step. Velt shows it under **Team → SAML → App-based → Sign-in links**.

<Note>
If you're already signed in to Velt, these links open the Velt Console without going to Okta. To test sign-in, sign out of Velt first or use a private window.
</Note>

## Set up user sync

Okta adds people to your Velt workspace when you assign them to the Velt app, and removes them when you unassign or deactivate them. This uses SCIM 2.0.

<Steps>
<Step title="Create a sync token in Velt">

Save your SAML configuration first. Then, on **Team → SAML → App-based → 4. Automatic user sync (SCIM)**, click **Turn on and create token** and copy the token.

<Warning>
Velt shows the token once. Treat it like a password and paste it only into Okta. If it's lost or shared, create a new one: the old one stops working right away.
</Warning>

</Step>
<Step title="Connect Okta to Velt">

In Okta, open the Velt app's **Provisioning** tab and click **Configure API Integration**:

1. Tick **Enable API integration**.
2. Paste the token into **API Token**.
3. Click **Test API Credentials**. Okta confirms the app was verified.
4. Leave **Import Groups** off. Velt doesn't sync groups.
5. Click **Save**.

</Step>
<Step title="Choose what Okta sends to Velt">

Under **Provisioning → To App**, click **Edit** and enable **Create Users**, **Update User Attributes** and **Deactivate Users**. If **Set password when creating new users** is on, turn it off. Click **Save**.

</Step>
<Step title="Assign people">

On the **Assignments** tab, assign people or groups. Okta adds each person to Velt within seconds. If people you assigned before turning on sync don't appear, unassign and assign them again.

</Step>
<Step title="Check the result">

In Velt, **Team → Users** lists everyone Okta added. On **Team → SAML → App-based → 4**, the status shows **On**, the last four characters of your token, and when Okta last connected.

</Step>
</Steps>

### What Okta changes in Velt

| In Okta you | In Velt |
|---|---|
| Assign someone, or add them to an assigned group | They're added to the workspace as a Member. Nothing changes if they're already a member. |
| Change their profile | Okta sends the change to Velt. |
| Unassign or deactivate them | They're removed from the workspace, and signed out if it was their only Velt workspace. |
| Reactivate or reassign them | They're added back. |
| Unassign the workspace owner | Nothing. Sync never removes the owner. |

Only emails on your workspace's Email Domain are synced. Sync never changes anyone's role; change roles under **Team → Users**. Velt doesn't email the people sync adds; they sign in from the Velt tile or your sign-in page.

### Manage user sync

- **Replace the token:** click **Create a new token** and confirm. The old token stops working right away. In Okta, go to **Provisioning → Integration → Edit**, paste the new token, click **Test API Credentials**, then **Save**.
- **Turn sync off:** click **Turn off** and confirm. Okta can no longer add or remove anyone. Current members stay.

## Making changes

- **Okta renewed its signing certificate:** on **Team → SAML → App-based → 3. Connect Okta**, import the same Metadata URL again and click **Save Configuration**. Do it before the old certificate expires.
- **New URL Slug:** change it in Velt, then in Okta under the app's **General → App Settings → Edit → Velt workspace URL slug**. Share the new sign-in link with your team.
- **Several Velt workspaces:** add Velt in Okta once per workspace, each with that workspace's URL slug. Give each a clear Application label, for example `Velt - Staging`.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| No **SAML** tab under Team | Only workspace owners and admins see it. Ask one of them to do the Velt steps. |
| No **App-based** option on Team → SAML | Velt isn't in the Okta catalog for your account yet. Use the [manual SAML setup](/security/saml-sso). |
| "SAML slug is already in use by another workspace" | Pick a different slug, for example `acme-corp`, and enter the same slug in Okta. |
| "This email domain is already used for SAML by a workspace outside your organization." | A workspace that belongs to another organization uses that domain. If you believe it's yours, contact Velt support. |
| "Public email domains cannot be used for SAML." | Use your organization's own domain, not a provider such as `gmail.com`. |
| Import doesn't fill in the Okta values | Copy the **Metadata URL** from the app's **Sign On** tab. It ends in `/sso/saml/metadata`. |
| Okta says the person can't use the app | They aren't assigned. Assign them on the app's **Assignments** tab. |
| *Contact your admin to sign in* after signing in with Okta | They aren't a member of the workspace. Invite them under **Team → Users**, or set up user sync. |
| *SAML is not set up for this email domain.* | The email doesn't match the workspace's Email Domain exactly. Check it on **Team → SAML → App-based**. |
| *Signed in as a different account* | Okta already had a session for someone else. Sign out of Okta or use a private window, then try again. |
| **Test API Credentials** fails in Okta | Check that sync is **On** in Velt and that you pasted the whole, newest token. |
| Someone you assigned isn't in Velt | Their email isn't on your Email Domain, or **Create Users** is off under **To App**. Okta lists failed pushes under **Dashboard → Tasks**, where you can retry them. |
| Some pushes fail while you assign a very large group | Velt accepts up to 600 sync requests a minute per workspace. Wait a minute, then retry the failed tasks in Okta. |
| Sign-in fails after Okta renewed its certificate | Import the Metadata URL again in Velt and save. |

Still stuck? Email [support@velt.dev](mailto:support@velt.dev) with your workspace name, your URL slug and a screenshot of the error. Never send the whole sync token.
