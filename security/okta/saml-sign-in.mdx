Add **Velt** from the Okta app catalog and your team can sign in to the Velt Console with Okta: from their Okta dashboard, or from Velt's sign-in page.

- **Protocol:** SAML 2.0
- **Setup time:** about 10 minutes
- **Your product:** the Velt SDK running in your product is not affected. This only changes how your team signs in to the Velt Console.

To add and remove people from Okta as well, set up [user sync](/security/okta/scim-user-sync) after this guide.

## How it works

1. You set your workspace's Display Name, Email Domain and URL Slug in Velt.
2. You add the Velt app from the Okta catalog and enter your URL Slug.
3. You copy the app's Metadata URL from Okta and import it in Velt.
4. You assign people to the app in Okta and invite them to your workspace in Velt.
5. Your team signs in from the Velt tile in Okta or from your workspace's sign-in page.

## Supported features

| Feature | Supported | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Sign in from Okta (IdP-initiated SSO) | Yes | People click the **Velt** tile on their Okta dashboard. No extra settings. |
| Sign in from Velt (SP-initiated SSO) | Yes | From your workspace's sign-in page, or by entering a work email on Velt's sign-in page. See [Signing in from Velt](#signing-in-from-velt). |
| Joining without an invite | No | Signing in with Okta never adds anyone to a workspace. Each person needs an invite in Velt, or has to be added by [user sync](/security/okta/scim-user-sync). |
| Roles from Okta | No | Change a member's role in Velt under **Team → Users**. |
| Single Logout | No | Signing out of Velt doesn't sign you out of Okta, and the reverse. |

## Prerequisites

- **An Okta admin account** that can add apps (Super Administrator or Application Administrator).
- **Owner access to your Velt workspace.** Only the workspace owner sees **Team → SAML 2.0** in the Velt Console.
- **Your company email domain,** for example `acme.com`. Everyone who signs in with SSO needs an email on this domain. A domain can be used only by workspaces that belong to your organization.

## Setup

<Steps titleSize="h2">

<Step title="Set up your workspace (in Velt)">

Sign in to the [Velt Console](https://console.velt.dev) as the workspace owner and open **Team → SAML 2.0**. Under **1. Your workspace**, fill in:

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Display Name** | Your company name, for example `Acme`. It's shown on your sign-in page and in the workspace list people see when they sign in. |
| **Email Domain** | Your company email domain without `@`, for example `acme.com`. It must match exactly: `mail.acme.com` counts as a different domain. |
| **URL Slug** | A short name for your workspace, for example `acme`: lowercase letters, numbers and dashes. It appears in your sign-in links, and you type it into Okta in the next step. |

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/okta/saml-2-workspace.png" alt="The Your workspace section on Team → SAML 2.0 in the Velt Console" />
</Frame>

</Step>

<Step title="Add Velt from the Okta catalog (in Okta)">

1. In the Okta Admin Console, go to **Applications → Applications → Browse App Catalog**.
2. Search for **Velt**, open it, and click **Add Integration**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Application label:** a name your team will recognize, for example `Velt`.
   - **Velt workspace URL slug:** the URL Slug from step 1. Velt shows it with a copy button under **2. Add Velt in Okta**.
   - **Application username format:** **Email**, or keep **Okta username** if your Okta usernames are email addresses. Velt identifies people by their email.
4. Click **Done**. Okta may ask you to confirm with Okta Verify.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/okta/saml-2-add-in-okta.png" alt="The Add Velt in Okta section on Team → SAML 2.0 in the Velt Console" />
</Frame>

</Step>

<Step title="Copy the metadata URL (in Okta)">

Open the new app's **Sign On** tab and copy the **Metadata URL**. It looks like `https://acme.okta.com/app/.../sso/saml/metadata`.

</Step>

<Step title="Connect Okta to Velt (in Velt)">

1. Back on **Team → SAML 2.0**, paste the link into **3. Connect Okta → Metadata URL from Okta** and click **Import**. Velt fills in Okta's Entity ID, SSO URL and signing certificate, and tells you when the certificate expires.
2. Click **Save Configuration**. You'll see *SAML configuration saved.*

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/okta/saml-2-connect-okta.png" alt="The Connect Okta section on Team → SAML 2.0 in the Velt Console" />
</Frame>

</Step>

<Step title="Assign people (in Okta)">

On the app's **Assignments** tab, click **Assign → Assign to People** or **Assign to Groups**. Only people assigned to the app can sign in to Velt with Okta.

</Step>

<Step title="Invite the people who should get in (in Velt)">

Okta decides who can sign in. Your Velt workspace decides who gets in: only people invited to it can open it, even after signing in with Okta. Invite them one of two ways:

- **Invite each person:** **Team → Users → Invite** in Velt, with the email they use in Okta. This works with any Okta plan.
- **Sync from Okta:** Okta adds people as soon as you assign them and removes them when you unassign them. It needs Okta Lifecycle Management. See [Okta User Sync (SCIM)](/security/okta/scim-user-sync).

**Check that it works:** in a private browser window, sign in to Okta as someone you assigned and invited, and click the Velt tile. You should land in your workspace in the Velt Console.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Signing in from Velt

People don't have to start from Okta. Each of these sends them to Okta and back into the Velt Console:

| Where | Address | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Your workspace's sign-in page** (recommended) | `https://console.velt.dev/saml/<your-slug>/login` | Enter your work email → **Continue with SSO** → Okta → Velt Console. Bookmark it and share it with your team. |
| **Velt's sign-in page** | `https://console.velt.dev/saml/login` | Enter your work email. Velt finds your company's SSO from the email domain. If your company has more than one SSO workspace, choose one from the **Workspace** list. |
| **Direct sign-in link** | `https://console.velt.dev/saml/<your-slug>/sso` | Goes straight to Okta, with no email step. Also shown on **Team → SAML 2.0 → Sign-in links**. |
| **The Velt tile on the Okta dashboard** | None | Works as soon as someone is assigned and invited. |

Already signed in to Velt? Then these links open the Velt Console without a trip to Okta. To test sign-in, sign out of Velt first or use a private window.

## Several Velt workspaces

- **Add Velt in Okta once per workspace,** for example Dev, Staging and Production. Each copy gets that workspace's URL Slug and is connected on that workspace's **Team → SAML 2.0** page. People see one tile per workspace, so give each a clear Application label, such as `Velt - Staging`.
- **Your workspaces can share one email domain,** as long as the same account owns them. When someone signs in by email, Velt lists the SSO workspaces they belong to, by Display Name.
- **The first time someone uses a second workspace's SSO,** Velt asks them to connect it: they sign in the way they usually do, in the same tab, and after that either one works.

## Making changes

- **Okta renewed its signing certificate:** on **Team → SAML 2.0 → 3. Connect Okta**, import the same Metadata URL again and click **Save Configuration**. Do it before the old certificate expires.
- **New URL Slug:** change it in Velt, then in Okta under the app's **General → App Settings → Edit → Velt workspace URL slug**, and share your new sign-in link.
- **Turn SSO off:** contact Velt support.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| No **SAML 2.0** tab under **Team** | Only the workspace owner sees it. Ask the owner to do the Velt steps. |
| *SAML slug is already in use by another workspace.* | Pick a different URL Slug, for example `acme-corp`, and enter the same slug in Okta. |
| *This email domain is already used for SAML by a workspace outside your organization.* | Another organization uses that domain. If you think it's yours, contact Velt support. |
| **Import** doesn't fill in the Okta values | Copy the **Metadata URL** from the Velt app's **Sign On** tab in Okta; it ends in `/sso/saml/metadata`. You can also paste the metadata XML itself. |
| Okta says the person can't use the app | They aren't assigned. Assign them on the app's **Assignments** tab. |
| *Contact your admin to sign in* | They aren't invited to the workspace. Invite them in Velt under **Team → Users** with their work email, or set up [user sync](/security/okta/scim-user-sync). |
| *SSO not configured* or *SAML is not set up for this email domain.* | The email doesn't match the workspace's Email Domain exactly, or the sign-in link has an old slug. Check both on **Team → SAML 2.0**. |
| *Connect ... SSO to your account* | That email already has a Velt account that signs in another way. Sign in that way in the same tab; Velt then connects SSO to the account. |
| The Okta tile stopped working after you changed the slug | Update **Velt workspace URL slug** in the Okta app's **General → App Settings**. |
| Sign-in fails after Okta renewed its certificate | Import the Metadata URL again in Velt and save. |
